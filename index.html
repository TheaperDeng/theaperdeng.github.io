Junwei Deng 邓珺玮

Ph.D. Student in Information Sciences · UIUC

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Jiaqi Ma. My research focuses on data-centric AI for LLM and foundation models, especially through principled methods of data attribution.

I work in Google Research as a Research Intern in Summer 2026. Previously, I was a Machine Learning Engineer at Intel (2020–2023) and a Research Intern at Microsoft MSRA (2025). I received my M.S. from University of Michigan and my B.S. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
